FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to install anything?
No — Pyrox Biz runs entirely in your browser. Just sign up, and you can start working from any device, anywhere. A mobile app is available on Android, and an iOS version is coming soon.
Can I import my existing data?
Yes. You can import your products, customers, suppliers, and opening balances from Excel or CSV files in minutes. Our team will also help you migrate from other systems for free on Business and Enterprise plans.
Is my data safe?
Absolutely. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We perform hourly automated backups and store them in geo-redundant data centers. Only you and the users you authorize can access your business data.
What happens after my free trial ends?
Your trial lasts 14 days. When it ends, you can pick a plan and keep going with no data loss. If you decide not to continue, your data stays safe and can be exported any time.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your dashboard with one click. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period, and we'll never charge you again.
Do you offer support in Urdu?
Yes — our WhatsApp support team responds in both English and Urdu, seven days a week, from 9 AM to 11 PM.
